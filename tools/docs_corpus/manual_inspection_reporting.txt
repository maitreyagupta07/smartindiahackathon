INSPECTION REPORTING MANUAL — Static Equipment (Vessels, Tanks, Piping)

Section 1: Report Structure
Every inspection report must contain the following sections in order:
Equipment ID, Inspection Date, Inspector Name, Inspection Type (internal /
external / thickness survey), Observations, Findings, Recommendations, and
Next Inspection Due Date.

Section 2: Thickness Survey Findings
When a thickness survey shows wall loss exceeding 10% of nominal thickness
since the last survey, the finding must be classified as "Priority 1" and
escalated to the Static Equipment Engineer within 24 hours, regardless of
whether the calculated remaining life still meets code minimums.

Section 3: Corrosion Under Insulation (CUI)
Insulated carbon steel piping and vessels operating between 10°C and 175°C
are classified as CUI-susceptible per API 583 and must be included in the
risk-based CUI inspection program, with insulation removed at a statistically
representative sample of locations during each turnaround.

Section 4: Approval Note Generation
Findings classified as Priority 1 or Priority 2 require a formal Approval
Note to be generated and routed to the Maintenance Planning group before any
repair work order is raised. The Approval Note must reference the original
inspection report number and summarize the finding, recommended action, and
required approval level (Shift In-Charge for Priority 2, Unit Head for
Priority 1).

Section 5: Recordkeeping
Digitized inspection reports, including scanned handwritten field notes,
must be stored against the equipment's unique tag number in the plant asset
register within 48 hours of report finalization.
