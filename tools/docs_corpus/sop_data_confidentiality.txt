SOP: Handling of Confidential Plant Data with AI-Assisted Tools

1. Purpose
This SOP governs the use of AI assistants for engineering, operations, and
administrative tasks involving plant data, to prevent unauthorized
disclosure of confidential information.

2. Policy
2.1 No plant document, drawing, inspection report, financial figure, or
personal data may be submitted to any AI tool or service hosted outside the
organization's own premises and network.
2.2 Only the organization's approved on-premise, air-gapped AI assistant may
be used for tasks involving confidential material.
2.3 Any output generated by the on-premise assistant (approval notes,
summaries, calculations, code) remains subject to the same review and
approval workflow as if it were produced manually.

3. Verification
3.1 The IT Security team shall periodically audit network logs of the
on-premise AI system to confirm zero outbound connections to public
internet endpoints.
3.2 Any detected external network call from the AI system is treated as a
security incident and reported immediately per the Incident Response SOP.
