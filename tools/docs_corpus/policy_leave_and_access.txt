TITLE: Internal Policy — Leave, Access Cards, and IT Assets

1. LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
1.1 Earned Leave (EL): 30 working days per calendar year for grades M1-M3;
    33 working days for grade M4 and above.
1.2 Casual Leave (CL): 10 days per calendar year, non-carry-forward.
1.3 Sick Leave (SL): 12 days per calendar year; a medical certificate is
    mandatory for any absence exceeding 2 consecutive days.
1.4 EL carry-forward is capped at 90 days accumulated. Days above the cap on
    31 December are encashed at basic pay.
1.5 Leave requests of 5 days or more must be filed at least 14 calendar days
    in advance and approved by the reporting manager and the division head.

2. ACCESS CARDS
2.1 Every employee is issued one proximity access card (Type A). Contractors
    receive a time-bounded Type C card valid for the contract period only.
2.2 A lost card must be reported to IT Security (extension 4700) within 24
    hours. The replacement fee is 500 currency units, waived once per
    employee per 3-year period.
2.3 Access levels L1-L4 are defined in HR-DIR-01. Elevation above an
    employee's grade default requires division-head approval and is
    reviewed every 6 months.
2.4 Card access to confined-space and hot-work zones is enabled only while a
    valid Permit to Work (SOP-PTW-01) is open for that person.

3. IT ASSETS
3.1 Laptop refresh cycle: 4 years. Earlier replacement requires an approved
    fault report (reference code IT-FR-xxxx).
3.2 No plant data may be copied to personal storage or personal cloud
    accounts — see SOP-AI-03 (data confidentiality).
3.3 Standard laptop storage allocation is 512 GB; an increase to 1 TB
    requires IT Security approval and is granted only for CAD/inspection
    imaging roles.
3.4 Software not on the approved catalogue (reference code IT-CAT-2026) must
    not be installed. Requests go through the IT service desk (extension 4711).
