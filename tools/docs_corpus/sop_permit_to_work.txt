STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Permit to Work (PTW) — General Plant Areas

1. Purpose
This SOP defines the mandatory steps for obtaining a Permit to Work (PTW)
before any maintenance, hot work, confined space entry, or electrical
isolation task is carried out within refinery process areas.

2. Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party personnel performing
work inside battery-limit process units, tank farms, and utility areas.

3. Procedure
3.1 The requesting engineer shall submit a PTW request at least 24 hours
before planned work, specifying: work location, nature of work, isolation
requirements, and estimated duration.
3.2 The Area Shift In-Charge shall verify isolation of energy sources
(electrical, mechanical, process) before countersigning the permit.
3.3 For hot work (welding, grinding, cutting), a Fire Watch must be posted
for the full duration of work plus 30 minutes after completion.
3.4 For confined space entry, atmospheric testing (O2, LEL, H2S, CO) must be
logged every 2 hours and the permit is void if any reading exceeds safe
threshold values defined in Annexure A.
3.5 On completion, the permit must be formally closed and signed off by both
the requesting engineer and the Area Shift In-Charge before the area is
handed back to operations.

4. Responsibilities
- Requesting Engineer: raises and closes the permit, ensures crew briefing.
- Area Shift In-Charge: verifies isolations, authorizes work start.
- Safety Officer: audits high-risk permits (hot work, confined space) daily.

5. Records
All PTW records must be retained for a minimum of 3 years and made available
for internal and statutory audits on request.
